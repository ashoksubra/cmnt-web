How to fill the gamaka spreadsheet
==================================

Files
-----
  melakarta-gamakas.csv   Default anuswara for each scale degree in arohana
                          and avarohana, for all 72 mēḷakartas.
  phrases.csv             Optional overrides when neighbours change the shape
                          (a turn, visesha sanchara, etc.).

Open either file in Excel or Google Sheets. Save as CSV (UTF-8) when you send
it back. Do not rename the column headers.

Columns (melakarta-gamakas.csv)
-------------------------------
  melakarta   1–72
  raga        Catalogue name (you may leave as-is)
  direction   aroha  |  avaroha
  swara       s  r  g  m  p  d  n  s'
              s' is tara Sa. Add extra rows later for s` (mandra) if needed.
  pattern     See notation below. Three kinds of cell:
                (blank)   not decided yet — playback keeps the ¼/¾ leap
                plain     intentionally no anuswara (straight note)
                sA gr     expansion, scaled to that note's duration
  notes       Free text for yourself (school, source, "only in slow kalam"…)

Columns (phrases.csv)
---------------------
  Same as above, plus:
  prev        Previous swara (s, r, s', …). Blank = any.
  next        Following swara. Blank = any.
  Lookup is prev>swara>next, then the direction default in
  melakarta-gamakas.csv.

Pattern notation (same as CMNT / the Sankarabharanam example)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  s r g m p d n     short swara (1 count)
  sA rI gA mA pA dA nI   long (2 counts) — capital 2nd letter
  s'  n`            tara / mandra stayi
  gr                two shorts run together (same as "g r")
  ,                 karvai, 1 count, holds the previous pitch
  ;                 karvai, 2 counts
  spaces            optional; "g r gA" and "grgA" parse the same
                    except two-letter longs (sA, gA) must stay together

Worked example — Sankarabharanam arohana (already filled in the sheet)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  s     plain
  r     sA gr
  g     g r gA
  m     gA m g
  p     p g pA
  d     pA s' d
  n     s' n s' n
  s'    p sA' ,

Avarohana for 29 is left blank for you. Other mēḷakartas are blank rows.

What to send back
-----------------
  The two CSV files (or a Google Sheet export of the same columns).
  Partial fills are fine — only rows with a pattern (or "plain") are loaded.
  Phrase rows are optional.

After it is in the repo, playback uses: written { }(tag) in the song first,
then a matching phrases.csv row, then aroha/avaroha for that swara, then
the ¼/¾ leap.
